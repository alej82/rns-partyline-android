RobotoEmoji.ttf and RobotoEmoji-Bold.ttf
========================================

These fonts are generated by merging two font families so Kivy can render
Latin text and monochrome emoji with one font:

- Roboto Regular/Bold, Copyright 2011 Google Inc., licensed under the Apache
  License, Version 2.0. See RobotoEmoji-Apache-2.0.txt.

- Noto Emoji, Copyright 2013 Google LLC, licensed under the SIL Open Font
  License, Version 1.1. See RobotoEmoji-OFL.txt.

The merged files are distributed with the same terms for their respective
portions. The font names are changed to RobotoEmoji; no Reserved Font Name from
Noto Emoji is used.

Source fonts:
- Roboto: https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Roboto
- Noto Emoji: https://fonts.google.com/noto/specimen/Noto+Emoji
